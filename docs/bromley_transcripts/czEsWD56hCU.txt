#         down when can't hit lower bound of range → stay in range
#  11:43  Variable loading is GOOD — boring to keep same weight all sets
#  12:24  RPE TARGET: recommend reaching RPE 10 on accessory work
#  12:38  Default range RPE 7-8 — don't out your spine going to failure
#  13:00  Block structure: 4 weeks at rep range, then adjust range or
